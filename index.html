Poetic lines for the beloved

Everytime I set my sights on you, time stopped as if God allow me to appreciate his masterpiece just a minute more.

When you smile, thousands of flowers grow jealous while millions of stars stand in awe.

Even the most skillful artists would failed trying to capture your beauty, and if success, all of masterpiece would seem unfinished.

Flowers bloom because spring arrives, but spring arrives because you smile. (Velvet Lines on facebook)

Time erase thousands of memmories, but every time it reached your beauty it forgot.

God painted the universe so beautifully, and yet he chose his favorite part and put it in your eyes.
